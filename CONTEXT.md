# Contract evidence model

Vocabulary for representing the agreements in this experiment.

## Language

**Clause**: A provision identified within the source agreement, often numbered. It can contain multiple sentences and multiple rights or obligations.

**Source passage**: An exact range of original document text used as evidence. It can cover a clause, preamble, signature block or attachment.

**Provision**: A structured interpretation of a statement in the agreement, such as a permission, obligation, restriction or declaration. Multiple provisions can be supported by one clause.

**Party role**: A company's capacity in one agreement, such as Principal or Distributor. The role is distinct from the company itself.

**Condition**: A stated circumstance that limits or triggers a provision; recording a condition does not establish that it has occurred.

**Generic scope**: A description of a set of things, such as orders accepted before termination. It does not identify a real order or establish that any such order exists.

**Event type**: A described kind of occurrence, such as termination or shipment. It is distinct from a recorded occurrence of that event.
